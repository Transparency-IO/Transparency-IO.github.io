Know who you’re evaluating.

Transparency detects and blocks unauthorized AI tools and other outside aids during the interview, so a strong technical screen reflects the candidate’s real ability.

Decide with evidence.

After each round, Transparency analyzes screenshots, transcripts, and other interview data, and gives your team clear, actionable feedback. Decisions get faster and more consistent.

Feedback your whole team can act on.

Every round ends with a report that ties each assessment to something that actually happened in the interview: a moment in the transcript, a screenshot, a flagged event. No gut-feel summaries, just evidence your team can review and compare across candidates.
